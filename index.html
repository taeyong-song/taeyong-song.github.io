I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Architecture and Architectural Engineering at Seoul National University. My research sits at the intersection of building physics, building performance simulation, and artificial intelligence.

I am interested in models that are not only accurate, but also physically meaningful and transferable when building data are scarce.

03 · Research

Projects

Physics-Embedded Thermal Modeling

Hybrid models that connect RC thermal dynamics with neural networks.

First-principles equations provide an interpretable thermal backbone, while neural networks learn parameters from limited measurements.

Data-Efficient Building Models

Transfer learning and model adaptation under scarce building data.

Knowledge learned from one building supports modeling in another while preserving physical plausibility.

Causal Models for Retrofit Decisions

Simplified models for transparent and robust building-energy decisions.

Causality-aware models preserve relationships that matter for retrofit decisions.
